**Data Flow & Automation (Must Be Crystal Clear & Formula-Driven)**

- ProgressReport.xlsx has its own independent input section but links dates and calculated percentages from the other documents where relevant
- All numbers must stay linked via formulas so that changing hours or prior billed values automatically updates downstream documents
